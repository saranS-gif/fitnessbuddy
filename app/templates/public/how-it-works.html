How FitBuddy AI Works

A closed-feedback generative loop designed to ensure continuous workout progression without burnout.

1

Biometrics & Goal Assessment

Our 8-step wizard collects your age, weight, target fitness objective, workout intensity, experience level, equipment setup, and preferred training days.

2

Gemini AI Generates 7 Structured Days

FitBuddy’s generative engine creates an evidence-based 7-day program specifying warm-ups, movements, sets, reps, rest intervals, and recovery protocols. A baseline revision is safely committed to the database.

3

The Adaptive Feedback Loop

When fatigue or schedule changes occur, simply type or tap quick feedback (e.g. "Add more cardio", "Make workouts easier", "I need another rest day"). Gemini recalibrates future days while your original plan remains intact.
